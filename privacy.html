Privacy Policy

NextAir mobile application
Effective date: 28 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 28 September 2026

北京高标物联科技有限公司 ("we", "us") operates the NextAir mobile application (the "App"), which lets you control compatible air-conditioning systems from your Android device. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the App only, not to any other product or website we may operate.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password — required to create and sign in to your account. Passwords are transmitted over an encrypted connection; we do not display them back to you.
  • Country / region code — determines which data centre your account belongs to. This is chosen by you at sign-up and cannot be changed afterwards.
  • Verification code — sent to your email for registration, sign-in and password reset.

Location information

  • Approximate and precise location — collected only while you are adding or commissioning a device, so the App can discover nearby Bluetooth devices and read the Wi-Fi network your phone is connected to. Location is not collected continuously and is not collected in the background.
  • You can deny the location permission at any time; device commissioning may then not work, but all other features will.

Device and usage information

  • Phone / tablet information: device model, operating system version, App version, system language, and diagnostic logs used to troubleshoot crashes.
  • Air-conditioner data: device name, device ID, virtual ID, MAC address, IP address, firmware version, on/off state, mode, fan speed, set-point temperature, zone names and zone damper states.

Photos and camera

  • With your permission, the App uses the camera to scan the QR code printed on your controller when adding a device, and may read images from your gallery to scan a saved QR code. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us.

We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS messages, precise advertising identifiers, or health data. The App contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDK.

2. Permissions the App requests

PermissionWhy

3. Who we share information with

The App is built on the IoT platform provided by Tuya Smart Inc. and its affiliates (Hangzhou Tuya Information Technology Co., Ltd.). Tuya acts as a data processor on our behalf: it stores and transmits your account and device data to make remote control work, and does not use it for its own purposes.

Disclosure required by our IoT platform provider (Tuya SDK)

Our IoT connectivity is delivered through the Tuya App SDK. Tuya's own SDK compliance terms require that we disclose this in our privacy policy, in the following terms:

"To provide interconnection services between IoT devices, our App uses the Tuya SDK to implement the basic functions of the product and service. Without the Tuya SDK integrated, you would not be able to use the corresponding smart product and service features. Through the Tuya SDK, the following functions are implemented:"

FunctionPersonal data collected
Account system Account or profile data: email address, phone number, username and login credentials, nickname, avatar, country code, language preference and time zone. Feedback submissions, which may contain your email address, phone number and the content of your feedback.
Home system When a home or device is created or used: your custom home name, device name, device ID and device location.
Device-related Device information: MAC address, IP address, wireless connection information, operating system type and version, app version number, push notification identifier, log files and mobile network information. Log data: SDK performance data such as MQTT connection rate and socket connection rate (de-identified operational data). Device location information.

We only invoke Tuya SDK services after you have agreed to this policy. Tuya processes this data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

Other recipients

  • Cloud infrastructure and messaging providers used by Tuya (for example Amazon Web Services for hosting and Twilio for email / SMS delivery of verification codes).
  • People you invite — if you use "Share device", the email address you enter and the devices you select become visible to that person.
  • Authorities — where required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where your data is stored

Accounts created in Australia and New Zealand are hosted in Tuya's European data centre; accounts in other regions are hosted in the corresponding regional data centre. Your information may therefore be processed in a country other than your own. Where required, international transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Details of Tuya's data centres are published on tuya.com/rule.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and device data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, the deletion takes effect after a 7-day cooling-off period — signing in again during those 7 days cancels the request. After deletion your personal data is removed from the active systems, except where we must retain records for legal, accounting or safety reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — view and edit your account details and device names inside the App.
  • Delete your account — in the App go to Me → Settings → Delete Account, or use our web form at https://jiangxining.github.io/nextair-legal/account-deletion.html. Both are always available, including if you no longer have the App installed.
  • Export or remove a device — unbind a device in the App at any time; its data stops being collected.
  • Withdraw permissions — revoke camera, location or photos permissions in Android settings.
  • Complain — if you are in Australia you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC); in New Zealand, the Privacy Commissioner.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections and stored with access controls and encryption at rest, consistent with the safeguards described in the Tuya Privacy Policy. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password for your account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the App or by email to your registered address before they take effect. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
