NextAir — Account Deletion / 账号注销说明

Effective date / 生效日期: 2026-10-23 · NextAir (com.gaobiaoiot.app.nextair)

NextAir complies with Google Play's account deletion requirements. If you created a NextAir account, you can permanently delete it at any time.
NextAir 遵守 Google Play 的账号删除政策。您可随时永久注销已注册的账号。

1. Delete your account in the app (recommended) / 在 App 内注销（推荐）

  1. Open the NextAir app and sign in.
    打开 NextAir App 并登录。
  2. Go to “Me” (我的) → “Settings” (设置).
    进入「我的」→「设置」。
  3. Tap “Delete / Cancel Account” (注销账号).
    点击「注销账号」。
  4. Confirm the cancellation.
    确认注销。

2. 7-day cooling-off period / 7 天冷静期

After you confirm cancellation, your account enters a 7-day (one-week) cooling-off period. Your account and all associated information will be permanently deleted only after this period ends.
确认注销后，账号进入 7 天（一周）冷静期，冷静期结束后才会永久删除账号及全部关联信息。

If you sign in again during the cooling-off period, the cancellation request is automatically cancelled and your account remains active.
若您在冷静期内重新登录，注销请求将自动取消，账号继续有效。

3. Can't access the app? / 无法使用 App？

You may also request deletion by emailing nextair-support@example.com from the address linked to your account. We will process the request within 7 days.
也可发送邮件至 nextair-support@example.com （使用注册邮箱），我们将在 7 天内处理。

4. Data deleted / 数据删除范围

Upon permanent deletion we remove your account profile, login credentials, device bindings and all personal data tied to the account. We retain only the minimum records required by law, if any.
永久删除后，我们将清除账号资料、登录凭据、设备绑定及全部关联个人数据；仅保留法律要求的极少数记录（如有）。

This page satisfies Google Play "Account deletion" disclosure.
本页用于满足 Google Play「账号删除」披露要求。
