Delete your NextAir account

Last updated: 28 September 2026

You can delete your NextAir account and the personal data associated with it at any time. Pick whichever method suits you — both do the same thing.

Option 1 — Inside the App (recommended)

Open NextAir and go to:

Me → Settings → Delete Account → Confirm

Your account is scheduled for deletion immediately. The App will sign you out.

Option 2 — By email (works even if the App is uninstalled)

Send us a deletion request from the email address registered on your account:

Request account deletion by email

Or email us directly at:

jiangxining0808@163.com

We will confirm receipt and process the request. We may ask you to confirm you own the account before deleting it.

What happens next

  1. Your account is scheduled for deletion.
  2. There is a 7-day cooling-off period. During this time the deletion is pending and your data is retained.
  3. If you sign in again during those 7 days, the deletion is cancelled and your account returns to normal. This protects you if the request was made on a lost or shared device.
  4. After 7 days the deletion is permanent: your account, device bindings, zone names and schedules are erased and cannot be recovered.
Before you delete: if you shared your devices with other people, that sharing ends. Your air-conditioner hardware is not affected — it simply returns to an unbound state and can be commissioned again later with a new account.

Deleting only some data

  • Remove a single device: in the App, open the device and choose Release device. That device stops being linked to your account; the rest of your data is untouched.
  • Stop sharing with someone: Share → select the person → Remove.
  • Uninstall the App to erase data stored locally on your phone. This does not delete your cloud account — use one of the options above if that is what you want.

Questions

Contact jiangxining0808@163.com. If you are in Australia and are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC); in New Zealand, to the Privacy Commissioner.
